Dot Line
An interactive type experiment

Type that makes room.

A field of yellow dots, with letters in the spaces between. Move through it and watch the dots find their way home.

Hover to push the dots. On touch screens, press and move. Every dot keeps its own space.

Dot size is the diameter as a percentage of the grid spacing.

Try a style

Put it on your website.

Plain JavaScript. Responsive. No dependencies.
